Capacity & Margin Dashboard

I took a public federal dataset covering roughly 5,500 hospitals and tested whether the capacity logic I use daily in pricing holds up in the healthcare industry. Patient days over available bed days is occupancy; revenue over available bed days is revenue per available unit; patient days over discharges is average length of stay.

Facilities running above 70% occupancy posted a positive median operating margin. Below 25%, the median was roughly negative 9%, and 70% of them lost money.

    MGM Resorts International

  • Pricing Decision & Performance Tool — Full Rebuild

    Excel · SQL · VBA · XML

    The primary tool my team uses to set pricing and monitor property performance needed some upgrades since it hadn't been changed in over 10 years. I led the redesign and rebuilt it largely on my own, becoming the go-to person for all additional/subsequent ad hoc tool modification requests.

    I spoke with many people from the pricing team to understand frontend requirements and worked with our data team to have them build gold layer data to be easily loaded.

    The tool is used daily by roughly 30 people across the revenue team: revenue managers, directors and executive directors, the group optimization team, the Senior Vice President of Revenue Management, and the Chief Revenue Officer.

    The tools I used: SQL for data retrieval, VBA for automating tasks when possible, XML for a custom ribbon & interface, and extensive formula work to improve relevant metrics for decision making.

  • Lead Time & Segment Analysis

    SQL · Data visualization

    An executive wanted to know which customer segments buy early and which ones wait until the last minute.

    I wrote a set of CASE statements in SQL to bucket transaction records into groups based on purchases by days before arrival (DBA), then built the visualization comparing segments across those groups The result showed which segments commit far enough ahead to be worth pursuing early, and which ones tend to purchase close to the date.
