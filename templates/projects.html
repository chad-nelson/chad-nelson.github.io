Capacity & Margin Dashboard

I took a public federal dataset covering roughly 5,500 hospitals and tested whether the capacity logic I use daily in pricing holds up in an industry I've never worked in. Every core metric has a direct equivalent: patient days over available bed days is occupancy; revenue over available bed days is revenue per available unit; patient days over discharges is average length of stay.

It holds up. Facilities running above 70% occupancy posted a positive median operating margin. Below 25%, the median was roughly negative 9%, and 70% of them lost money. The relationship is clean and it runs in one direction across the entire range.

I cleaned the raw file from 6,103 records down to 5,507 usable ones, built the calculated fields and weighting logic, and designed a three-page report with filters for region, size, facility type, and ownership. It's public — click through it yourself.

    Pricing Decision & Performance Tool — Full Rebuild

    MGM Resorts International

    The primary tool my team uses to set pricing and monitor property performance had become slow and manual to maintain. I led the redesign and rebuilt it largely on my own.

    I gathered requirements from the managers who use it daily, worked with our data team to confirm what sources were available, and rebuilt it from the ground up: SQL for automated data retrieval, VBA for the processing logic, XML for a custom interface, and extensive formula work behind the summary views. I tested it with end users, fixed what broke before rollout, and documented it in SOPs so it doesn't depend on me being around.

    In daily use by roughly 30 people across the revenue organization — revenue managers, directors and executive directors, the group optimization team, and up through the Senior Vice President and Chief Revenue Officer.

    Lead Time & Segment Analysis

    MGM Resorts International

    An executive wanted to know which customer segments buy early and which ones wait until the last minute. The analysis was the easy part — the data wasn't stored in a way that could be queried by lead time at all.

    I wrote a layered set of CASE statements to bucket raw transaction records into time-to-purchase bands, then built the visualization comparing segments across those bands. The result showed which segments commit far enough ahead to be worth pursuing early, and which ones only ever show up close to the date.

    Bundled Pricing Automation

    Allegiant Air

    One product was sold at a discount when it was purchased alongside a second product. That protected the bundle but quietly ate the margin on the transaction as a whole.

    I built an automated adjustment that detects the bundle at the point of pricing and raises the first product's rate accordingly, so the full rate is preserved instead of discounted twice. I led the build because I had more depth in Python than the rest of the team and could get more out of the approach.
