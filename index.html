About

Professional and personal life

For the last five years, I've worked in pricing and forecasting. I'm now in a newly formed position on the pricing team at MGM Resorts International that allows me to combine my analytics and technology skills to help the pricing team in revenue management make the most informed decisions: SQL pipelines that pull, clean, and deliver data with minimal manual work/edits; extensive Excel reporting with formulas using VBA for automation; and SOPs so my team can answer their own questions as needed.

I am a mediator between the frontend decision makers and the backend data teams. I have experience in both sides to know how to build tools & reports that work for everyone.
